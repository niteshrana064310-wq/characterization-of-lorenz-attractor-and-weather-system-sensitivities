### About the Experiment 
The Lorenz Attractor is a mathematical model used to study chaotic systems and their sensitivity to initial conditions. It demonstrates how small differences in initial conditions can lead to significantly different outcomes over time.
This experiment focuses on understanding the behavior of the Lorenz system, visualizing the Lorenz attractor, and studying its relationship with weather system sensitivities. It helps explain why long-term weather prediction is challenging, even when the governing equations are known.
